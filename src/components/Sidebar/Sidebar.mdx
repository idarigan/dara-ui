## Features

- ✅ Collapse/expand with Ctrl+B
- ✅ Groups with expand/collapse
- ✅ Nested sub-menus
- ✅ Icon-only mode when collapsed
- ✅ Content panel with fade animation
- ✅ RTL support

---

## Keyboard Shortcut

Press `Ctrl+B` (or `Cmd+B` on Mac) to toggle the sidebar collapse state.
