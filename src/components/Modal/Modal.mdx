## Features

- ✅ Glass-heavy styling with blur(30px)
- ✅ Scale-in animation
- ✅ React Portal rendering
- ✅ Focus trapping
- ✅ Escape key support
- ✅ Backdrop click to close (configurable)

---

## Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ Do

- Use modals for important actions
- Keep modal content focused
- Provide clear actions
- Use `onConfirm` for primary actions

### ❌ Don't

- Don't stack multiple modals
- Don't put too much content in a modal
- Don't use modals for navigation
